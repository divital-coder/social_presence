# social_presence

A personal knowledge base: a list of a bunch of social networks including some of the
giant cybernetic collectives out there, plus tools, tasks, deadlines, ideas, and people.
Organized by purpose for fast retrieval in Neovim.

## Quick Jump

- [Deadlines](action/deadlines.md) - applications, programs, conferences
- [Tasks](action/tasks.md) - active to-dos and catch-up
- [Social Hub](reference/social-hub.md) - handles, communities, job boards
